## Testing 123

***The Thinker*** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): ***Le Penseur***), by [Auguste Rodin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auguste_Rodin), is a [bronze sculpture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronze_sculpture) depicting a [nude male figure of heroic size](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroic_nudity), seated on a large rock, leaning forward, his right elbow placed upon his left thigh, with the back of his right hand supporting his chin in a posture evocative of deep thought and [contemplation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contemplation). This universally recognized expression of "deep thought" has made the sculpture one of the most widely known artworks in the world. It has become the iconic symbol of *thinking*; images of the sculpture in profile are often used to indicate [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) and other practices of contemplation or [introspection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Introspection).
